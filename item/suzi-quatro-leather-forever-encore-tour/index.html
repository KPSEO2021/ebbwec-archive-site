Suzi Quatro — Leather Forever Encore

Main TheatreOne night onlyOn sale

Suzi Quatro returns to regional Victoria as part of the Leather Forever Encore Tour, with a programme drawn from the catalogue and a handful of deep cuts the touring band have built around for this run.

Box office →

About the tour

The Leather Forever Encore Tour is the second leg of Quatro's late-career return to regional Australian touring, following the original Leather Forever run that crossed the country in late 2024. The encore leg refines the setlist and revisits a number of the venues that sold out the original run within a week of going on sale. The Playhouse was one of those venues and the encore performance is the second appearance Quatro has made at the Main Theatre on this tour cycle.

Quatro's touring band is the lineup that has accompanied her across recent European and UK tours. The current bass setup retains the Fender Precision and pedal chain that defines the live sound, with two guitars and a stripped-back rhythm section.

The setlist

The setlist is anchored by the singles that defined the early career, including 'Can The Can', '48 Crash', 'Devil Gate Drive' and 'If You Can't Give Me Love'. The mid-set ballad section draws from the broader catalogue, and the encore returns to the high-energy material that opens the show.

Booking

One performance in the Main Theatre. Tickets through the box office. The Playhouse seating chart is fully reserved. Booking opens to subscribers two weeks before public on-sale and the show is expected to sell out across the standard pricing tiers; restricted-view seats and the small number of stalls returns will be released in the week of the performance.
