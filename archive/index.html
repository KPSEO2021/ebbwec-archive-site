Archive

Past productions.

The Playhouse keeps a permanent archive of past productions as a record of the programming history. Cast lists, programme notes, and audience response where it was collected.

Recent archive entries

Boys In The Band, 2015

2015 community theatre season · Studio · four performances. Community theatre production of Mart Crowley's 1968 play. Sold out three of four performances; one of the strongest community theatre productions the Studio has hosted.

Archive policy

The full archive is held on permanent record and individual production records are restored and reformatted for the public archive when staff time permits. Productions are listed by the season they ran in. Each archive entry includes the production photography that the production company has cleared for public release.

If you appeared in a production whose archive entry is not yet on the site, or you have a query about the cast list or production credits, the contact page has the right address.
